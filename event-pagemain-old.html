ABOUT US

What is MakeX?

The MakeX National Competition is your gateway to innovation, teamwork, and international recognition. Young minds from across the country will compete, create, and showcase their talents to qualify for the MakeX World Championship. This competition promotes STEAM Education (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Mathematics), empowering participants to think critically, build creatively, and prepare for the challenges of the future.

Past Events

01 — MWRC NATIONALS 2024

Mumbai Nationals

Where India’s Young Innovators Compete at the Highest Level The MakeX 2024 National Championship brought together some of India’s brightest young robotics enthusiasts for a high-stakes celebration of technology, engineering, and innovation. Hosted at Kanakia International School, Mumbai, the championship united 500+ participants, 250+ teams, and 50+ schools from across India — creating a national platform where ideas were transformed into intelligent machines and ambition into achievement. Across intense robotic challenges, teams put their engineering precision, programming expertise, strategic thinking, and teamwork to the test. Every mission demanded more than just a working robot — it demanded innovation under pressure.

500+ Participants 250+ Teams 50+ Schools

02 — MAKEX INTERNATIONAL 2023

UAE Championship

A GLOBAL STAGE FOR YOUNG INNOVATORS The MakeX International Robotics Competition brought together talented young innovators from around the world in Abu Dhabi, UAE, creating a truly international stage for robotics, engineering, and innovation. Hosted at Erth, Abu Dhabi, the competition challenged teams to push the boundaries of what their robots could achieve through precision, strategic thinking, programming, engineering, and teamwork. With teams representing different countries and diverse approaches to robotics, the event was a powerful exchange of ideas, skills, and competitive spirit — bringing the global MakeX community together under one roof.

International Teams Global Competition Abu Dhabi, UAE

03 — MakeX 2022

NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP

WHERE INDIA'S ROBOTICS JOURNEY TOOK THE NEXT STEP The MakeX 2022 National Championship brought together young robotics enthusiasts from across India for a landmark national competition in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Teams stepped onto the national stage to compete in challenging robotic missions, putting their engineering skills, programming, strategic thinking, creativity, and teamwork to the test. From designing and building their robots to executing complex missions under competition pressure, the championship gave students an opportunity to turn their technical knowledge into real-world solutions — and experience the intensity of competing at a national level.
