About MakeX India

MAKEX INDIA

MakeX India is part of a premier global STEAM robotics competition platform dedicated to inspiring young minds. We bridge hands-on creation and real-world problem solving, encouraging youth to apply Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM) through thrilling robotics challenges, innovation showcases, and international exchange events.
